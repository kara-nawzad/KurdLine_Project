HOW THE WRAP WORKS
  1. 1 · RDI generation. Every node transmits RDI (Remote Defect Indication) on the control channel. A broken span stops RDI flow — both end nodes detect silence in < 10 ms.
  2. 2 · Block decision. End nodes exchange WTR-block messages on the healthy side. No control-channel ambiguity — the ring is unidirectional, so wrap is deterministic.
  3. 3 · Clockwise wrap. SUL-01 and KIK-01 simultaneously switch transceivers: traffic SUL → EBL → KIK. Switchover completes in 38 ms, well under the 50 ms fast re-route target.
  4. 4 · SLA audit. Path 98 → 398 km · one-way delay 0.49 → 1.99 ms · optical penalty absorbed by the 3.5 dB span margin (Module 3).

REL-17 DOPPLER COMPENSATION
  • ±38.5 kHz S-curve: radial velocity crosses zero at pass zenith — the MT pre-compensates carrier frequency by the ephemeris-predicted offset; the residual is tracked by the receiver PLL inside ±180 Hz.
  • Masking loss: the Zagros canyon mask (default 30°) clips both ends of the pass. Raising the mask to 40° costs up to 4.0 min of usable contact — traded against blockage probability in the valley.
  • TA extension: LEO slant range 100–560 km stretches round-trip TA to 1.83 ms at the horizon (zenith 0.67 ms) — 3× the LTE-terrestrial maximum, hence the Rel-17 extended TA grid.
CHOMAN OUTPOST — TERMINAL & MOUNT
  • Terminal: 45 cm parabolic dish, 34.0 dBi @ 2.0 GHz · 600 W LNA (NF 0.8 dB) · MT. Halgurd sector mount, azimuth ±120° sweep.
  • Ephemeris: TLE-derived pass list pushed to the gateway each 6 h; Choman geometry gives ~2 passes/day, max elevation 78° — well clear of the 30° ridge mask.
  • Service model: 220 Mbps S-band return for SCADA + voice + 4K video; ATSSS (5.3) steers this access around the core fiber and 7 GHz MW tiers.

STEERING DOCTRINE — 3GPP 23.501
  • Mode 1 · Per-traffic-flow steering: eCPRI C-RAN flows ride the core DWDM (8.0 Tbps) first; the LEO NTN flow carries only when the ground tiers degrade.
  • Mode 2 · Load balancing: 7 GHz MW absorbs up to 2.4 Gbps during fiber maintenance windows — the 420 min outage budget holds without touching the 99.999% MW window.
  • Failover ordering: fiber (99.99 %) → MW (99.999 % w/ SD) → LEO (99.5 %). Dual-failure drill (button) forces LEO-only: 220 Mbps sustains SCADA + voice, 4K video sheds.
  • Policy anchor: 35 % of core fiber capacity is policy-reserved for KRG emergency services during peak hours — ATSSS never allocates that slice to consumer flows.
